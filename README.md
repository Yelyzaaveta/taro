# Programmer's Tarot & Runes

**Know the future of your code.**

A brutally honest fortune-telling app for developers. Draw a tarot card or throw runes — get a prediction about your code, your deploy, and your sanity.

---

### What is this?

A web-based tarot & runes reader with **78+ tarot cards** and **24 Elder Futhark runes**, all with programmer-themed meanings. Every card has an upright and reversed interpretation — from triumphant deploys to mass production disasters.

### Cards include

- **Major Arcana** — The Fool (junior pushing to main), The Magician (full-stack wizard), Death (project rewrite)
- **Minor Arcana** — Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles — all in dev context
- **Chaos cards** — Friday Deploy, Copy-Paste God, Console.log Adept, Legacy Necromancer, Docker Overlord, and many more

### How to use

1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Pick **Tarot** or **Runes** mode
3. Click the deck / throw button
4. Read your fate
5. Cry or celebrate accordingly

### Tech stack

Pure HTML + CSS + JS. No frameworks. No dependencies. No node_modules. Just vibes.

### 30% chance

Every card has a **30% chance** of being drawn reversed — which usually means things are about to go very wrong.
